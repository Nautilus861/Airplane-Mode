# Airplane-Mode
This repo will act as the source of truth for all documentation regarding project Airplane Mode.
***STATUS:*** Concept development and preliminary design.

# Introduction
"Project Airplane Mode" is a challenge, an opportunity to apply a chronically under-utilized understanding of the fundamentals of mechanical engineering, and most of all a monument to hubris.

While technically there is no need that drives an end product - no consumer good to market and profit from or government contract to win - this project will be treated as though there were, and therefore needs system requirements, deliverables, schedules, documentation and success criteria. That all lives here.

# Motivation
Why am I doing this? While I was busy driving across the entire continental United States, my phone broke. Suddenly and unexpectedly. Being without this tool I have become so dependent on was frustrating and a little scary. I use it for all of my banking, communication, navigation (especially when 99% of the week long trip was through territory completely unfamiliar to me), entertainment etc.

While indeed an unfortunate moment for this to occur, it did provide an opportunity for much reflection. I was left with one question: Why am I trusting one of the most expensive, fragile, and critical items I own to a product with all the sophistication of a plastic spoon.

I'm talking of course about my phone case. Relying on what amounts to some rubber overmolding attenuating a fall from a staggering 5 feet and 11 inches seems foolish. While the malfunction that afflicted my phone was not related to falling, with my replacement in hand I have vowed to protect it from this eventuality.
